This site is my public notebook, portfolio, and small web lab in one place.

## What I wanted

I wanted something that could hold different kinds of work without feeling like a generic portfolio template:

* writing
* project case studies
* playful experiments
* personal pages that still feel like they belong to the same site

Astro made that split clean. Most pages stay content-first and static, while the few interactive pieces can opt into React only where they need it.

## How it is built

The core stack is Astro, MDX, React, and TypeScript running on a self-hosted VPS with Docker and Caddy.

That gives me a few things I care about:

* fast page loads for mostly-static content
* content collections for blog posts and project entries
* isolated client islands for interactive widgets
* low operational overhead

I also wired in Keystatic so the content layer can grow into a less code-only workflow over time.

## Tradeoffs

The site has a lot of room to grow because I intentionally started with a small surface area. That keeps iteration easy, but it also means every new interactive feature needs some discipline around persistence, abuse prevention, and SEO. A personal site still becomes a real production app once strangers can write to it.

## What changed recently

One of the biggest upgrades was treating project entries like actual case studies instead of flat cards. The content already lived in MDX, but it was effectively hidden. Exposing that content made the portfolio more honest because it shows process, not just screenshots and links.
