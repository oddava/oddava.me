# AniShows got a big update !!

If you've visited AniShows before, (i bet u haven't) you might remember the Browse page looking a little.. overwhelming. A giant "Discover Anime" hero header, a wall of filter buttons, a sidebar crammed with options, and a grid of cards that just kind of dumped everything at you at once. It worked, but it wasn't exactly pretty. And navigating it? Not the smoothest experience.

I've been sitting on the urge to redesign it for a while, and I finally did. Here's everything that changed.

---

## The Discovery Page, Rebuilt From Scratch

The old Browse page tried to do too much at once. Every filter was visible, every option was front and center, and the result was a page that felt more like a spreadsheet than a place to find your next favorite anime.

The new one takes a completely different approach. Instead of a generic grid dump, the page now has curated sections – **Trending Now**, **Popular This Season**, and **Upcoming Next Season** – each with a horizontal scroll row and a "View All" link if you want to dig deeper. It's much easier to land on the page and immediately know what to click.

The filter bar is still there, but cleaned up significantly. Search, genres, year, season, format, and airing status are all neatly laid out at the top without overwhelming you. Advanced filters are tucked behind a toggle for those who want them. Simple.

## New Anime Cards

The cards got a redesign too. They're bigger, cleaner, and actually let the cover art breathe. The old ones felt a bit cluttered with metadata, the new ones surface only what matters at a glance, with a cleaner hover interaction.

## UI & Design Overhaul Across the Board

The discovery page wasn't the only thing that changed. The overall design of AniShows got a refresh too, more visual consistency, a cleaner color scheme, and better spacing throughout the site. The mobile experience in particular got a lot of love: there's now a bottom tab bar for easier navigation, and the profile page was redesigned with a Twitter-inspired layout, including support for custom accent colors.

It's one of those updates where nothing is drastically different in terms of features, but everything just *feels* better to use.

---

## Why Now?

Honestly? The old design was starting to bother me every time I opened the site. When you're the one who built something and you still flinch at your own UI, it's time to fix it.

I also wanted the site to feel more like a place people actually want to spend time, not just a functional tool, but something with a bit of personality. Still a work in progress, but this update moves it in the right direction.

---

## What's Next

There's still a lot I want to add and improve. More community features, better tracking, and some things I'm not ready to announce yet. But for now, the foundation feels solid and I'm genuinely happy with how it looks.
