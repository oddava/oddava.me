## Media and content cautions

- `public/images/books/why-we-sleep/`, `atomic-habits/`, and note media exist. Mockups use original inline SVG/CSS objects instead of republishing third-party art whose licence is not established by the repository.
- `diary/20-07-2026.md` links to `reading/manhwa/the-greatest-estate-developer`; there is no corresponding exported Markdown document. Do not manufacture one from image assets.
- The diary index and metadata updates are not a reliable latest-entry ordering mechanism. A future rebuild should use existing document identity and content dates carefully.
- The displayed Why We Sleep material is the author's personal reading summary. This package makes no independent medical claim and adds no book quotation.
